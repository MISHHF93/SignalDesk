# SignalDesk Product Law

## 1. Product Law: One Business. One Page. One Operational View.
- SignalDesk is an AI-Orchestrated Business Operating System, not a disjointed collection of dashboards or tabs.
- The daily operating experience must remain one intelligent Command Center.
- Supporting pages may exist for setup/billing/audit, but daily investigation and action happen through progressive disclosure: inline expansion, contextual drawer, sheet, modal, overlay, or Command Bar interaction.
- Order of operations: **GLANCE → INSPECT → ACT → VERIFY**.

## 2. High-Value Attention Center
- **Center**: Needs Attention, Waiting on Me, Unresolved Decisions, Approvals, Failed Actions, High-Materiality Signals, Active Missions.
- **Periphery**: Healthy connectors, routine logs, background infrastructure. Healthy systems should be quiet.

## 3. The 5 User Efforts
Every major change must reduce at least one of:
1. Searching
2. Understanding
3. Deciding
4. Coordinating
5. Executing

If a feature adds settings, noise, or maintenance without reducing effort, simplify or eliminate it.
